# Bid Ladder — Research Standards

## Launch Market Research Questions

1. Which ecosystem has the highest density + reachability + cohesion?
2. What existing competitive behavior exists for visibility?
3. What content/distribution channels can drive repeat visits?
4. What is the willingness to pay for visibility in this ecosystem?
5. Who are the earliest likely participants?

## Method

- Primary evidence preferred (interviews, surveys, public data).
- Secondary evidence cited.
- No fabricated market size numbers.
- Label all claims FACT / HYPOTHESIS / ASSUMPTION.

## Output Format

Research findings use:

```text
OBSERVATION

EVIDENCE

IMPACT

RECOMMENDATION

PRIORITY

SCOPE

CONFIDENCE
```

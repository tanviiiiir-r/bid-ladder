# Bid Ladder — Design System Contract

## Principles

- The board is the product. UI must surface discovery immediately.
- Movement is visible. Rank changes, visits, and overtakes are prominent.
- Status is shareable. Rank cards and share prompts are first-class.
- Mobile-first. Most discovery will happen on mobile/social.
- No dark patterns. Real signals only.

## Components (Tentative)

- Leaderboard row
- Category filter
- Listing card / detail
- Share card
- Admin review queue
- Movement badge

## Open Questions

- Brand colors/typeface (DECISION-002 / Founder).
- Exact interaction patterns for overtakes and live updates.

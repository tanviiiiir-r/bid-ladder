# Bid Ladder — Company Identity

## Mission

Create a live competitive discovery platform where people immediately understand what is getting attention inside a specific ecosystem, and where companies or projects can earn or compete for higher visibility through legitimate activity.

## Product Category

Regional discovery + competitive attention platform.

## Core Loop

```text
Discovery → Interest → Listing → Position → Competition →
Attention → Sharing → New users → More competition
```

## Product Promise

- The board is the product.
- Competition must be real, never fabricated.
- Status is valuable and easy to share.
- Movement creates retention.
- Discovery comes before monetization.
- Simplicity over feature parity.
- Experiments over assumptions.

## What Bid Ladder Is NOT

- A generic business directory.
- A conventional advertising network.
- A task/reward marketplace.
- A social network.
- A cryptocurrency product.
- A clone of Outbid.lol.
- An enterprise analytics dashboard.
- A generic SaaS application.
- A fake popularity/ranking system.

## Strategic Hypotheses

| Hypothesis | Evidence Needed | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Launch with a specific internet-native ecosystem (AI/SaaS/startups) | Market density, reachability, cohesion | Open |
| Leaderboard is the primary product | User behavior tests | Open |
| Status and competition drive retention | Retention metrics | Open |
| Economic values must be discovered experimentally | Experiment outcomes | Open |

## Operating Principles

1. Inspect before acting.
2. Distinguish facts from assumptions.
3. Avoid duplicating existing work.
4. Preserve project memory.
5. Prefer experiments over guesses.
6. Keep the product simple.
7. Protect financial integrity.
8. Protect user data.
9. Escalate major decisions to the founder.
10. Document important decisions.

## Information Classification

All important claims must be labeled as one of:

- **FACT** — verified and sourced.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — testable prediction.
- **ASSUMPTION** — believed true, not yet verified.
- **EXPERIMENT** — actively being tested.
- **DECISION** — approved and recorded.
